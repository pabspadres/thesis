BIG FASHION

SMALL ETHICS?

How well do you know the brands you shop?

The moment we buy an item of clothing, we are part of the clothing industry; therefore, we should know how our clothes are being made.

This project explores the ethical compromises of large-scale fashion brands in America through data analysis & visualization.

DISCLAIMER

The data and resulting scores in this project are used for exploratory and educational purposes, and should not be interpreted as definitive ratings of any brand.

LET'S START WITH A GAME

When you play, you will find an axis, where the horizontal line is our ethical compliance score, the vertical line is the company's size in revenue scale. You will be given 6 brands of this project and you have to drag and drop them within the axis where you think they are meant to be based on the intersection of the two measurements.
