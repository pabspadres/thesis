ETHICS

The moment we buy an item of clothing, we are part of the clothing industry; therefore, we should know how our clothes are being made.

This project explores the ethical compromises of large-scale fashion brands in America through data analysis & visualization.

To evaluate this, 31 large-scale American brands have been analyzed through their social and environmental performance.

Quantifiable data from multiple sources has been sorted into 5 different unique categories.

Social Transparency, Labor Compensation, Human Rights Risk & Responsiveness, Environmental Impact, and Supply Chain Transparency.

Together, these categories serve as metrics to build the Ethical Compliance Index which rates each brand with a score.

HOW ARE BRANDS SCORED?

Learn more about how 5 metrics are used to create the ethical compliance score that rates brands.

Ethical Compliance
Index

The ethical compliance index is calculated by multiplying each metric score by its weight, and adding all five up to produce a score from 0-100.
